Privacy Policy

Last updated: 30 October 2026

This policy explains how the Siddhivinayak Enterprise app ("the app") handles information. The app helps a business owner record worker attendance, advance payments and salary.

Information we store

  • Owner: email address and password (password is handled by Google Firebase Authentication; we cannot see it).
  • Business records entered by the owner: worker names, login IDs, daily wage, attendance, advance and salary records.
  • Workers: a login ID and PIN created by the owner. A worker can see only their own attendance, advance and salary.

How information is used

Information is used only to run the app: to sign users in and to show them their records. We do not sell your data, show ads, or share it with third parties for marketing.

Where it is stored

Data is stored on Google Firebase (Authentication and Cloud Firestore) and is protected by access rules, so each business can see only its own data.

Deleting your data

To delete your account and all records, email us from your registered email address and we will delete it. Owners can also remove workers inside the app; their past records stay with the owner's business data.

Children

The app is meant for business use by adults and is not directed to children.
